About Matamong Guest House

Matamong Guest House is a newly established guest house, opening its doors in December 2024. We are dedicated to providing a cozy, luxurious, and safe environment for all our guests.

“Experience comfort, style, and the heart of Maseru all in one stay!”

Our mission is to become one of the top guest houses in Maseru by continuously improving every week and month. We aim to offer exceptional services, modern rooms, and a memorable experience for every visitor.
